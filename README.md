## 🌉 Overview

**Smriti Setu** --- meaning *"Bridge of Memory"* --- is a mobile-first
cognitive assistance platform designed to support older adults
experiencing memory challenges while helping caregivers stay connected
with everyday cognitive activities, routines, reminders, and progress.

The product is designed around a simple principle:

> **Technology should adapt to the person --- not force the person to
> adapt to technology.**

Smriti Setu combines gentle cognitive activities, multilingual
interaction, accessibility controls, personalized progress tracking, and
caregiver support into one approachable experience.

### 🎯 Designed for

-   Older adults who benefit from structured cognitive engagement
-   Families and caregivers who want visibility into daily activities
-   Remote and underserved communities where accessible digital support
    can be valuable
-   Users who need larger controls, readable typography, voice
    assistance, and language flexibility

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## ✨ Why Smriti Setu?

Many digital cognitive tools are built around generic exercises and
standard interfaces. Smriti Setu takes a more human-centered approach by
bringing together:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Challenge                           Smriti Setu Approach
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Difficult-to-use interfaces         Large touch targets and simplified
                                      navigation

  Vision/accessibility barriers       Font scaling, high-contrast mode
                                      and voice read-aloud

  Language barriers                   Multilingual interface with
                                      regional language options

  Repetitive cognitive activities     Multiple game categories and
                                      difficulty levels

  Caregiver uncertainty               Linked patient profiles and
                                      activity monitoring

  Daily routine support               Reminders for cognitive sessions
                                      and everyday activities

  One-size-fits-all engagement        Personalized activity and
                                      baseline-oriented progress views
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🌐 Multilingual + Culturally Aware

Smriti Setu is designed with the cultural and linguistic diversity of
Northeast India in mind.

The product direction emphasizes:

**Language → Familiarity → Engagement → Consistent participation**

Regional language support can help reduce the barrier created when older
adults are expected to interact with technology only in English.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🔐 Privacy & Responsible Use

Smriti Setu is designed around responsible cognitive assistance.

### Principles

-   Use individualized profiles rather than publicly exposing personal
    information
-   Use caregiver connection codes for linking profiles
-   Present performance as activity/baseline information rather than
    diagnosis
-   Keep accessibility and user control visible
-   Avoid presenting cognitive-game results as a substitute for
    professional medical assessment

> **Disclaimer:** Smriti Setu is a cognitive-assistance and
> caregiver-support application. It is not a medical diagnostic device
> and should not be used as a replacement for professional healthcare
> advice.
